Install

No app store.
Just add it.

Blip installs straight from your browser and opens like any other app, with its own icon and notifications.

Open Blip to install
  1. Android and desktopOpen Blip in Chrome and tap Install, or choose Add to Home screen from the menu.
  2. iPhone and iPadOpen Blip in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Allow notificationsSo reminders reach you, even with Blip closed.
